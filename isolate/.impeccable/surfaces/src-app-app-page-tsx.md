# CP Platform app surface

- **Mode:** Operate
- **Audience:** CP artists, programme managers, coordinators, and leadership
- **Job:** See what needs attention, coordinate work, record revenue, and report progress without leaving a spreadsheet-like workflow
- **Primary action:** Move work forward or record the next meaningful update
- **Visual direction:** Closer to the public Collective Perspectives site: high-contrast ink and paper, direct hierarchy, coral as a scarce signature accent, and clear human-centered language
- **Must remain:** Existing routes and product behavior; Kanban, task list, Gantt, sales, reports, notifications, and SGD/en-SG conventions
- **Must avoid:** Generic purple SaaS gradients, decorative card repetition, color-only status, tiny functional text, and motion that obscures state
- **Responsive requirement:** Desktop shell with intentional mobile navigation, stacked summaries, accessible filters, scrollable dense data, and preserved chart meaning
